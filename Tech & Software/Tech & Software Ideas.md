# Tech & Software Ideas

## Major Platform Concepts

### Global Flows
Visualise how money, people, trade, and power move between countries.
- Country-to-country: FDI, trade, commodities, people, oil, alliances, debt, military sales, conflict
- Filter by: geography, time slider, colour-coded industries
- Correlate news stories to data in time
- Show govt decisions and their results
- Companion: separate window to compare selected countries with graphs

### Product Provenance
Where does a product's money actually go?
- Worker pay (retail + factory), input costs, executive pay, owners/investors — broken down by country
- Environmental costs: transport, water, CO₂
- Show like-products side by side with money breakdown
- Social shopping: rank companies by ethical/social rating, show profile of good and bad
- Support local/minority businesses; filter by region
- Co-op retail site: local sellers listed, searchable by region
- Barcode scanner (phone/mounted camera) → product details
- Scan bank transaction history → see where your money went

### News Platform
Action-oriented, reliable news aggregation.
- Display headlines from many publications on one story + sentiment analysis
- Localized accounts of events — local journalists
- Reliability aggregation (cf. Politifact)
- Government + company decisions with context
- People tracker: what each politician / business leader / lobbyist did this week
- Alert system: notify when a specific event, place, or person becomes active
- Conveying scientific papers accessibly

### Privacy-First Tech Ecosystem
- **Anonabook** — anonymous social network (self-hostable pages)
- **Camera Technica** — anonymous photo sharing
- Anonymous video site
- **Foundit** — private news
- **SwissCows-style** — encrypted search engine
- Private phone OS
- Secure browser
- Secure peer-to-peer comms: designed to link to closest node possible
- Tor + private search with different encoding
- LAN protocols: create more LANs to avoid ISPs
- Neighbourhood networks with distributed data centres
- Local library as hypervisor and access point

### Neighbourhood Tech Infrastructure
- Local pool to cool supercomputer
- Differencing algorithm — store most of site on local drive
- Distribution of smaller servers: local → regional → national → global
- **Mlab, Ripe NCC, Endace** — network interface cards

## Sports Tracking Wearables

Platform for measurable coaching feedback across sports:

### Device Lineup
- Watch (main)
- Ring
- Elbow cuff
- Polo with shoulder sensor points
- Hat
- Belt with hip points
- Knee cuff
- Ankle straps

### Sport Applications
- **Golf**: score tracking, swing improvement, grip/stance correction, expected distance per club, azimuth camera
- **Tennis / Badminton / Ping-pong**: glove on other hand for left/right/high/low cues, belt for hip distance, measure wrist spin, 2 cameras to capture flight
- **Cricket**: swing and bowling analysis, baseball equivalent
- **Football**: ankle tracking — kicking and running acceleration
- **Other**: military training, swimming, athletics, weights with accelerometer + connect to watch and machines for stats and competition

### Garmin App Store
- Distribute via Garmin app ecosystem
- Legal: Atlassian-style licensing
