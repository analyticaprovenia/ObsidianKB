# Political & Social Vision

## Core Framework — Self-Sufficient Communities

A hierarchy of needs to be met locally, not corporately:

1. **Shelter** — government housing replacing mortgage and rental markets
2. **Power** — solar self-sufficiency replacing energy companies
3. **Water** — local water systems
4. **Telecom** — co-op OLPR replacing telcos
5. **Food** — community gardens replacing grocery chains
6. **Transport** — walkable cities, bikes, e-scooters reducing fuel and obesity
7. **Education** — schools with food tech, gardening, feed kids breakfast + lunch
8. **Healthcare**
9. **Protection** — legal drugs eliminate drug crime, virtually eliminate theft, reduce drunk driving

## Product & Consumption Policy

### Proprietary Rights Reform
- Products should only have 5 years of proprietary protection — government takes over after
- Car parts and aesthetics must be replaceable (5-year proprietary maximum)
- Phones: replaceable chip, screen, battery
- Clothes recycling infrastructure
- Construction recycling
- Schools to teach repair skills

### Complete Cost Visibility
- Show the complete cost of all goods: money + energy
- Flow of money, energy, value, labour, people

### Labour Theory of Value (Working Notes)
> Labour is value and money is a measure of value, so hoarding money is the accumulated time — in years — of human effort. Your money cost so many people their time, and could this be given to others in order to save them having to work for that time.

## Defense & Infrastructure Policy

### Smart Transport Infrastructure
- License plate QR codes (readable by traffic cameras, not by people)
- Contactless / RFID driver's licence
- Agents / doormen in every building
- Mini airports on motorway off-ramps (every 20km)
- Tunnels-from-tunnels distributed network
- Enforce generic design for all large/medium transport trucks
- Boats with drone launching capability

### Distributed Defense (Notes)
- **AWACS**: loitering AWACS array (3.5m × 600mm), Luneburg lens, chaff
- **Air superiority**: loitering jet with 1–2 air-to-air missiles
- **SAM**: distributed, disguised, passive + active
- **Anti-ship**: GPS-guided
- **AtA drones**: 500kph + air-to-air missile
- Small stealth drones — asymmetric warfare
- Shahid-X equivalents on standard trucks

Make NZ the Switzerland of the Pacific — neutral, prosperous, self-sufficient.
